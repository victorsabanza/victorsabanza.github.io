I attended the 30th ACS Green Chemistry & Engineering (GC&E) Conference in San Antonio, where I had the honor of receiving the [GCIPR Data Science and Modeling for Green Chemistry Award](https://acsgcipr.org/saturn-rxn-generative-molecular-design-with-steerable-and-granular-synthesizability-control-for-greener-chemical-synthesis/) together with my colleague [Jeff Guo](https://guojeff.github.io/). 🏆

I also had the pleasure of presenting our work on Saturn-RXN, showing how synthesis constraints and green chemistry principles can be incorporated directly into generative molecular design. It was a wonderful opportunity to share our research and exchange ideas with the green chemistry community.
